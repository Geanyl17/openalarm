OpenAlarm is a free and open-source alarm clock that makes you prove you're awake before it turns off.

<b>Missions</b>
To turn off an alarm with a mission, you have to complete it:
• Math: solve arithmetic problems, from sums of small numbers to problems like 17 × 6 + 38.
• Memory: repeat a pattern of lit tiles on a 3×3 grid.
While you work on a mission, the alarm turns quiet. Stop for 20 seconds and it gets loud again. An alarm with a mission can't be dismissed from its notification.

<b>Alarms that always ring</b>
• Rings at the exact minute, full screen over the lock screen.
• Set again after a reboot, a clock or time-zone change and an app update, and daylight-saving changes are handled.
• Rings even before the phone has been unlocked for the first time after a reboot.
• If the app is killed while an alarm rings, a backup alarm rings it again within a minute.
• Never silent: if the chosen sound can't be played, a bundled sound takes over.
• No way around it: leave the ringing screen and it comes straight back, and the volume can't be turned down. Emergency calls and phone calls always come first.

<b>Set it up your way</b>
• Scroll wheels for the time, with "Rings in 7 h 32 min" underneath, so a wrong hour or AM/PM stands out.
• Repeat days, labels, vibration, and a volume that rises gradually.
• Any alarm sound on your phone, including your own.
• Snooze for 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes, or any length up to an hour.
• A color and a cover photo for each alarm, with text that stays readable in any color.

<b>Private</b>
No internet permission, no ads, no tracking and no account. Everything stays on your phone.
